Body sheet

A demo of the tracker: body areas, tabletop ability scores, milk, mana and curses. Each button does what the AI's hidden tags or your commands would do in the script. Fat and muscle are tracked per body part, so training and eating change different areas. Nothing here touches AI Dungeon.

Chest detail

Support

Sizes are per breast. Fat follows your calories and your fat pattern. Glandular tissue does not respond to food or training, only to the story. Sag builds from fast changes and from heavy volume, and support halves it.

Milk

Potential

Supply follows how much you drain, up to what the glandular tissue can make. Draining more often than the tissue supports (over about 90% of full output) makes it grow slowly toward its potential. After a week of low demand it shrinks slowly back to where it started. Producing milk costs about 0.8 kcal per ml, and full breasts leak, slow down and add sag strain.

Mana

Each area stores mana in its muscle, fat and glandular cells, with glandular tissue holding by far the most per kg. Half of it fades every day, and anything past capacity is wasted. Filled areas glow blue on the figure and give temporary ability bonuses: Strength from arms, chest and legs, Dexterity from core and glutes, and a smaller boost to Constitution, Intelligence and Wisdom from the total.

Curses

HungerNeeds 25% more calories every day.
Mana leechSoaks mana into one area every day. Overflow turns into lasting tissue.
Forced milkLactation stays on, supply never drops below 60%, and the glandular ceiling is 50% higher.
Growth biasNew fat lands mostly in one area, and it leaves that area last.

Abilities

Tabletop scale: 10 is average and every 2 points is one step of modifier. Strength, Dexterity, Constitution and Charisma come from the body. Intelligence and Wisdom come from the story.
